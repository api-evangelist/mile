Mile is an AI-native supply chain execution platform for brands and distributors that own their inventory and fulfill orders directly to customers. It unifies Order Management (OMS), Warehouse Management (WMS), and Transportation / last-mile delivery (TMS) in a single system, with MAC, an AI agent that acts on exceptions rather than only flagging them. Mile exposes a partner REST API at lastmile.milenow.com covering orders, customers, products, categories and brands, promotion bundles, routes, drivers, vehicles, warehouse inbound orders, Aramex shipping, order optimization, and configurable webhooks for order-status, order-creation, settlement, and inventory-transfer events.

**Areas:** Company · Logistics · Supply Chain · Last Mile Delivery · Order Management · Warehouse Management · Transportation Management · Fulfillment · Shipping · Webhook · Route Optimization

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Mile: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
